\begin{frame}{Challenges of Non-negative Neural Networks}
  Non-negative training involves different dynamics in the optimization process
  due to the constraints that are enforced upon trainable parameters.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item Current available methods target \textbf{partial non-negative architectures}
  \item Applied on \textbf{small datasets} or \textbf{non-traditionally used ANNs}
  \item \textbf{Facing difficulties in scaling} ability and \textbf{generalization} in DL architectures
  \item Results in \textbf{performance degradation}
  \end{itemize}  
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Typical initialization}
  Initialization schemes set weights using a distribution that maintains
  a high activation variance. This prevents neuron saturation and ensures
  consistent gradient flow during backpropagation, mitigating the phenomenon of
  vanishing gradients.

\begin{frame}{Non-negative Optimization}
  Optimization algorithms can be modified accordingly to ensure that the network
  preserves the non-negativity of the parameters during training.

  \begin{table}[t]
    \centering
    \begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}[t]{>{\mystrut}XX}
            \toprule
            (U.0) \textbf{Update} & \(\theta_t =  \sigma_{nn} \left(\theta_{t-1} - \eta  \frac{\partial L}{\partial\theta_{t-1}} \right)  \) \\
            \midrule
            (U.1) Clip & \( \sigma_{clip}(x) = \max\{0,x\}\in \mathbb{R}_{+}\) \\
            (U.2) Absolute & \(\sigma_{abs}(x) = |x| \in \mathbb{R}_{+}\) \\
            \hdashline
            (U.3) Sigmoid & \( \sigma_{sig}(x) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-x}} \in (0, 1)\) \\
            (U.4) Squared & \(\sigma_{sqrt}(x) = x^{2} \in \mathbb{R}_{+}\) \\ 
            (U.5) Softplus & \(\sigma_{sft}(x)=\ln\!\left(1 + e^{x}\right) \in \mathbb{R}_{+}\) \\
            \bottomrule
        \end{tabularx}
    \vspace{-10pt}
  \end{table}
